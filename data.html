Transit Accessibility in San Diego County

This project evaluates public transit stop accessibility by analyzing infrastructure features, environmental barriers, and accessibility conditions using GIS and street-level imagery.

Dashboard About Data Sources Research Methods Team SDG Connections

Data Sources

Transit and spatial datasets

  • SANDAG GIS Data — Public transit stop and station data for San Diego County, including bus, commuter rail, trolley, and light rail stops managed by MTS and NCTD.
  • City of San Diego Transit Stops — Transit stop location data used to map and assess stop accessibility across the county.
  • City of San Diego Bike Routes — Bike route information used in the connectivity component of the accessibility score.
  • OpenStreetMap (Geofabrik) — Street and sidewalk-related spatial information used to support stop-level accessibility analysis.
  • Overpass Turbo — Tool used to extract OpenStreetMap features relevant to stop accessibility.
  • North County Transit District GTFS Feed — Transit schedule and route data used to inform transit stop accessibility analysis.
