Transit Accessibility in San Diego County

This project evaluates public transit stop accessibility by analyzing infrastructure features, environmental barriers, and accessibility conditions using GIS and street-level imagery.

About the Project

Many people rely on public transportation for daily mobility, but barriers at transit stops can prevent equitable access. This project examines stop features across San Diego County to identify opportunities for improving accessibility and creating more inclusive transportation spaces.

Our group, TransportSD consists of Sebastian, Devon and Amy who are leading the way in this project. Utilizing the ArcGIS public transit routes dataset and Google Street View, Transport SD will take a further look into the setup of public transport stops in San Diego by mapping public transportation stops, including images of what they look like, and giving the stops a score based on a pre-defined formula of accessibility.

Meet our team on the Team page. While we recognize that the public transportation system in the County of San Diego could benefit from general improvements, starting with small improvements of the locations at which people have to wait could increase the accessibility of our public transportation system greatly. Many vulnerable populations in cities rely on public transportation to get around. However, if the stops themselves present physical and environmental challenges, people may be discouraged or prevented from taking public transportation. Increasing the accessibility of a public transportation system contributes towards SDGs of Good health and well-being, reduced inequalities, and sustainable cities and communities by making these spaces more inclusive for everyone.
