Transit Accessibility in San Diego County

This project evaluates public transit stop accessibility by analyzing infrastructure features, environmental barriers, and accessibility conditions using GIS and street-level imagery.

What Are the Sustainable Development Goals?

The Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs) are a collection of 17 global goals established by the United Nations to address major social, economic, and environmental challenges around the world. Together, they provide a shared framework for improving quality of life while supporting more equitable and sustainable communities.

The goals recognize that issues such as health, inequality, transportation, housing, environmental sustainability, and economic opportunity are interconnected. Progress toward one goal can therefore contribute to progress in others.

This project contributes to that broader framework by examining accessibility within public transportation. Understanding the physical conditions of transit stops can help identify barriers that may affect people's ability to safely and reliably access transportation and the opportunities connected to it.
