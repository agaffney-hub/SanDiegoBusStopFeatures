Transit Accessibility in San Diego County

This project evaluates public transit stop accessibility by analyzing infrastructure features, environmental barriers, and accessibility conditions using GIS and street-level imagery.

SDG Connections

This project aligns with Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs):

  • SDG 3: Good Health and Well-being
      • This goal emphasizes the importance of ensuring healthy lives and promoting well-being for all. By improving transit accessibility, we can enhance access to healthcare services, reduce health disparities, and promote active transportation options that contribute to overall well-being.


  • SDG 10: Reduced Inequalities
      • This goal focuses on reducing inequalities within and among countries. By addressing barriers to public transit, we can promote equitable access to transportation for all individuals, regardless of their physical abilities or socio-economic status. This contributes to reducing disparities and fostering inclusivity in urban mobility.


  • SDG 11: Sustainable Cities and Communities
      • This goal focuses on making cities and human settlements inclusive, safe, resilient, and sustainable. Accessible public transit is a key component of sustainable urban development, as it reduces traffic congestion, lowers carbon emissions, and improves quality of life for residents.
