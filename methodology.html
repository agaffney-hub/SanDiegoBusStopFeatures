Transit Accessibility in San Diego County

This project evaluates public transit stop accessibility by analyzing infrastructure features, environmental barriers, and accessibility conditions using GIS and street-level imagery.

Methodology

We want to use visual data, as well as crowdsourced data, to evaluate the accessibility of public transit stops in San Diego County. We will use a combination of GIS data and Google Street View images to assess the physical and environmental characteristics of each stop. Our methodology involves identifying key features that impact accessibility, such as the presence of sidewalks, benches, shelters, and curb cuts. We will also consider environmental barriers, such as traffic volume and speed, lighting, and visibility. By analyzing these factors, we aim to provide a comprehensive assessment of transit stop accessibility in the region.
