Transit Accessibility in San Diego County

This project evaluates public transit stop accessibility by analyzing infrastructure features, environmental barriers, and accessibility conditions using GIS and street-level imagery.

About Data Sources Research Methods Results Team SDG Connections

About the Project

We evaluate accessibility at more than 6,000 transit stops across San Diego County, examining physical access, boarding, comfort, and connectivity.

What We Found

The countywide average accessibility score is 4.6 out of 10. Scores vary across regions, highlighting opportunities for targeted improvements.
